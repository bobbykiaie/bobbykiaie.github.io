# Set by Set

Set by Set is an iPhone and Apple Watch strength-training app that helps people build weekly workout plans, log sets and effort, track progress and per-exercise cardio response, and receive adaptive coaching. Its core planner and workout tools work without an account; optional iCloud, Apple Health, Apple Watch, and Bluetooth heart-rate connections remain under the user's control.

## Common Questions

### Is an account required?

No. Set by Set's core planner, exercise library, workout logging, and progress features work without a Set by Set account.

### How do I manage health connections?

Open Set by Set, tap the **Settings** tab, then choose **Health Connections**. Apple Health is optional, and you can change its access in iOS Settings or the Health app.

### How do I connect Apple Watch or a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor?

Start a workout and open the heart-rate menu. Apple Watch tracking can be enabled or disabled there. To use a compatible Bluetooth Heart Rate Service sensor, start a scan and choose the nearby device. Live samples and saved cardio-response insights stay on your device.

### How do I remove my data?

Delete individual routines and workouts in Set by Set, disconnect optional providers, or delete the app to remove its local data. Synced routines and exercise-library edits are managed through your private iCloud account and Apple's iCloud controls. Workout history, progress, notes, effort ratings, and heart-rate insights are not synced through iCloud.
