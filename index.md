# Set by Set

Set by Set is an iPhone and Apple Watch strength-training app that helps lifters build single-week or multi-phase workout plans, log sets and effort with auto-saved drafts, track weekly volume on a 3D muscle figure, share or rate community plans, compete with friends on consistency and effort, and receive adaptive coaching. Its core planner and workout tools work without an account; optional iCloud, Apple Health, Apple Watch, Bluetooth heart-rate, and social features remain under the user's control.

## Common Questions

### Is an account required?

No. Set by Set's core planner, exercise library, workout logging, auto-saved drafts, and progress features work without a Set by Set account. Optional Sign in with Apple accounts unlock cloud-synced profile sharing, plan publishing and ratings, and free friend competitions.

### How do friend competitions protect my privacy?

Competitions upload only qualifying workout counts, session duration, set counts, and personal-record flags needed for leaderboard points. Heart rate, cardio-response metrics, Apple Health data, bodyweight, raw weights/reps, and private workout notes are never uploaded.

### How do I manage health connections?

Open Set by Set, tap the gear on **Today** to open Settings, then choose **Health connections**. Apple Health is optional, and you can change its access in iOS Settings or the Health app.

### How do I connect Apple Watch or a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor?

Start a workout and open the heart-rate menu. Apple Watch tracking can be enabled or disabled there. To use a compatible Bluetooth Heart Rate Service sensor, start a scan and choose the nearby device. Live samples and saved cardio-response insights stay on your device.

### How do I remove my data?

Delete individual routines and workouts in Set by Set, disconnect optional providers, or delete the app to remove its local data. With iCloud sync on, your plans, custom exercises, exercise-library edits and a copy of your workout history (exercises, sets, reps, weights, effort ratings and notes) are kept in your private iCloud account and managed through Apple's iCloud controls. Heart-rate information is left out of that copy and stays on your iPhone.
